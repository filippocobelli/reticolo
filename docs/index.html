Reticolo

v0.2.1 · free

The free, local manager for your 3D model libraries. Everything you have scattered across folders, drives and the NAS, in one place, with thumbnails, a 3D viewer and one-click slicing.

Download for MacApple Silicon · macOS 12+ Download for Windowsx64 · Windows 10/11

Free. No account, nothing uploaded, your files are never moved or renamed. All releases · Ko-fi

The app’s interface is in Italian for now.

What it does

Library

Local folders, external drives and NAS shares, watched for changes. Thumbnails from the files themselves or rendered in 3D. ZIP, RAR and 7Z archives browsed like folders, without extracting.

Organise

Tags, collections and notes that follow the file even when you rename or move it. Hide what you don’t need: hiding an archive hides everything inside it.

Search

Instant search on names, paths, tags, notes and AI descriptions, optional semantic search, and a global shortcut to bring Reticolo forward.

3D viewer

Seven views, point-to-point measurements, screenshots, Bambu/Orca multi-plate projects, STEP assemblies with their parts, G-code layer by layer.

Duplicates

Exact copies, identical geometry even in another format, and probable duplicates for you to review. Nothing is ever deleted.

Printing

Detects 23 FDM and resin slicers and opens files in the one you choose, or drag them in. Print queue with time and filament read from sliced files.

Tools (beta)

3MF converter between Bambu, Orca, Prusa and generic 3MF, keeping plates and colours. Nameplate and vase generators with STL/3MF export.

Your way

Dark, light or automatic theme and the accent colour you like. Optional AI tags with OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Ollama or Codex. Automatic backups.

Installing

macOS. Open the .dmg and drag Reticolo to Applications. The app isn’t signed with an Apple certificate, so before the first launch run this once in Terminal:

xattr -cr /Applications/Reticolo.app

or go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

Windows. Run the installer. If SmartScreen appears, choose More info → Run anyway.

Private by design

Reticolo works offline. No account, no telemetry, no uploads: your files are only read. The AI features are optional, off by default and use your own key; only the thumbnail and file name are sent, and only when you ask.
